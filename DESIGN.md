# Design Specification: Ricetta (Tuscan Osteria Cookbook Editorial)

- **Product:** International recipe explorer and personal recipe bookmarking web app powered by TheMealDB API.
- **Visual Direction:** Warm Tuscan Osteria Cookbook Editorial (Semolina Paper `#FAF6EE`, Crisp Parchment `#FFFFFF`, Espresso Ink `#211915`, Tuscan Olive Herb `#3A5A40` primary CTA, Saffron Ochre `#8A5A00` culinary tags; Cast Iron Dark Mode `#161412` + Olive Sage `#86B88D`). All 9 color pairs verified WCAG AA >= 4.5:1.
- **Typography:** `Playfair Display` serif for recipe & section titles, `DM Sans` for legible cooking instructions, `IBM Plex Mono` for ingredient quantities and counts.
- **Three Dials (Antislop):** ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1.
